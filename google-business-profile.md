## Setup choices

- **Business name:** Orion's Belt (Google rejects names with extra keywords, so leave off "AI" or "Jefferson City")
- **Business type:** Service-area business. Hide the street address, since clients do not visit an office.
- **Service areas:** Jefferson City, MO; Columbia, MO; Cole County; Boone County; Callaway County; Osage County; Miller County; Moniteau County
- **Website:** https://orionsbelt.dev
- **Appointment link:** the same booking link the site uses (currently a placeholder, see the README)
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, or whatever Clark actually keeps. "By appointment" also works.

> Orion's Belt helps small businesses in Jefferson City and Central Missouri put AI to work. We start with an AI Checkup that finds where your team loses time and ranks what to fix first, with hours and dollars saved for each item. From there we train staff on the AI tools they already pay for, build fixed-price automations that connect email, QuickBooks, Google Drive, and your CRM, and set up an AI front desk that answers calls, books appointments, and texts back missed calls. Monthly support plans keep everything running. We work with law and government affairs firms, accountants, insurance agencies, nonprofits, healthcare practices, trades, retail, and agriculture. Fixed prices, no long contracts.
